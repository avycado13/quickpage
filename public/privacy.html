Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 24, 2026

Overview

quickpage is a personal dashboard that connects to your Google account to display your emails, calendar events, tasks, and Google Classroom assignments in one place.

Information We Collect

When you sign in with Google, we access the following data through the Google APIs:

  • Your name, email address, and profile picture
  • Google Tasks
  • Google Calendar events
  • Google Classroom courses and coursework

How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to display it within the quickpage dashboard. We do not sell, share, or transfer your personal data to any third parties.

Data Storage

quickpage operates entirely in your browser. Your Google access token and basic profile information are stored in your browser's local storage. We do not store your data on any server.

Third-Party Services

quickpage uses Google OAuth 2.0 and Google APIs. Your use of these services is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Data Deletion

You can revoke quickpage's access at any time from your Google Account permissions. Clearing your browser data will remove all locally stored information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
